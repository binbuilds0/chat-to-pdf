Save a ChatGPT or Claude chat as PDF or Markdown

Paste the conversation and get a clean PDF, with every message labelled and code, tables and math intact. It runs in your browser, so nothing is uploaded.

  • Free, no limit
  • No watermark
  • No sign-up
  • Nothing uploaded

Open the chat or its share link, press Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C, and paste here. Or open a file: a saved page, Markdown, or conversations.json from a ChatGPT or Claude data export.

2. Check the details
3. Save

In the print window, choose Save as PDF as the destination. Text in the PDF stays selectable and searchable.
